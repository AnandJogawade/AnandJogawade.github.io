About Me

Lead Penetration Tester

I am a Penetration Tester and Security Researcher with experience assessing web applications, APIs, mobile applications (Android & iOS), and enterprise infrastructure across both internal and external environments. My work spans black-box, gray-box, and white-box assessments with a strong focus on manual testing, business logic vulnerabilities, authentication flaws, privilege escalation, and attack chaining.

I assess Active Directory environments, network architectures, trust relationships, and privilege boundaries to uncover lateral movement and domain compromise opportunities. I evaluate security controls by testing endpoint protection, EDR/XDR solutions, and detection capabilities against adversarial techniques.

Security research is a core part of my work. I perform vulnerability research, source code review, and responsible disclosure. My research has resulted in publicly assigned CVE-2026-48849 and security fixes in widely used software. Beyond finding vulnerabilities, I collaborate with engineering and IT teams to provide risk-based remediation guidance and validate fixes through retesting.
